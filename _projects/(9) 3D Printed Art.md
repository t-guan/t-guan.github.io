# CAD Creations

After falling in love with 3D printing, I started to utilize my design skills to create little gadgets and doodads for friends. It started with simple quality of life improvement items, like headphone and phone stands, but started to create designs based on characters from film, television, and video games for friends as gifts.

I also started to learn more organic 3D Modelling using tools like Blender to create props and costumes for cosplay.

At the suggestion of some friends, I started to put some of these online for sale. Here's some shots of my first product, a Studio-Ghibli Soot Sprite inspired Yarn bowl!
